SSANZ

Trial entry tools

The interfaces on this page automate and simplify the process of taking your downloaded trial entries, extracting the data you need, and reformatting into various different forms that you may want to use for your trial. At each step the spreadsheet tools give your file back with new tabs added, so none of your original data is lost. The Trial Documents step produces the various formats of running orders in Word, judging sheets as a PDF, and will also print certificates.

IMPORTANT - trial lists are produced in a random order. If you wish to move people around, you should do that manually before proceeding to step 4. Remember to move the entire line. Also, if you want to change the running order for different elements, duplicate the entire tab and reorder them there.

The tools, in the usual order

  1. 1

    Payment Split

    Splits entries into Paid, Part Paid and Not Paid tabs and shows who still owes, and how much.

    Adds: Paid · Part Paid · Not Paid tabs

    Open →
  2. 2

    Email List

    Makes one email address per person, ready to paste into BCC. Run it on a Payment Split file to email just one group, or on your selection of trial groups. After a ballot it lists who has a place and who is on the waitlist.

    Adds: Emails tab · BCC list

    Open →
  3. 3

    Trial Lists

    Makes a randomly ordered running list for each trial (e.g. NWAdv1), with D / H reactivity flags and a check for handlers with two dogs in one trial. For an oversubscribed trial it can run the SSANZ ballot and keep a numbered waitlist.

    Adds: One tab per trial

    Open →
  4. 4

    Trial Documents

    Turns the trial tabs into Word documents (A4): running orders for the catalogue, callboards, and time stewards' and judges' sheets with one element and judge per sheet. It also prints the judging sheets as a PDF, one per dog for each search, with the date, host, judge, element, search time and running-order number filled in. And it prints the certificates onto the pre-printed SSANZ cards. Wherever possible data need only be entered once, however there are plenty of customisation options too, including the possibility of adding your logo.

    Makes: Running orders · Callboards · Time stewards · Judges (.docx) · Judging sheets · Certificates (.pdf)

    Open →
  5. 5

    Results Sheets

    Fills in the official SSANZ results spreadsheet for each class: organisation, Org Ref ID, date, venue, Trial #, judges and every dog in alphabetical order of handler, so the admin team only types the points and times. The SSANZ sheet keeps its lock and formulas, and extra rows are added for big classes.

    Makes: one SSANZ results sheet per class (.xlsx)

    Open →

You don't have to use every tool. Steps 1–3 each work on their own with an entries download; Trial Documents needs the file from Trial Lists, and Results Sheets needs the file saved from Trial Documents plus the SSANZ results files zip.

Good to know

  • Using Google Sheets? Choose File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx), drop that into a tool, then bring the result back with File → Import.
  • Old .xls files aren't supported. Open them in Excel and use Save As → Excel Workbook (.xlsx) first.
  • Chaining tools: each tool's download keeps all your tabs, so drop it straight into the next tool.
  • Needs internet to load the page. The work itself happens on your computer.
Found a problem? Report it

These tools are being tested. If something goes wrong, looks odd or is confusing, tell us here. Your report opens in your email app, ready to send. Nothing is sent until you press Send there.

Please don't attach your entries file or type anyone's name, email or other details. Describe the problem instead, for example “row 14 of NWAdv1” or “the third dog on the Judges sheet”.
