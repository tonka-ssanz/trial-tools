Good to know

  • Only certain trials lists every trial column with at least one entry, e.g. NWAdv1 (Nosework Advanced, trial 1). Tick one or several, or use the quick buttons to tick a whole trial. Anyone entered in any ticked trial is included.
  • Phone comes from the download's “Entry Phone” column, written in international form with no spaces, e.g. 021 792 551 becomes +6421792551, whatever way it was typed. If one email has two different numbers, both are shown.
  • Entered in shows which trials each person is in (only the ticked ones, if you picked trials). Someone with two dogs gets one row listing both dogs' trials.
  • Tab names follow your choice: Emails for all entries, or e.g. Emails NWAdv1 or Emails Trial 1. The top of the tab says exactly who is included. Drop the downloaded file in again to add a list for other trials alongside it.
  • Running the same choice again replaces that tab rather than adding a copy.
  • Email just one payment group: run Payment Split first, then pick the Paid, Part Paid or Not Paid tab here. You can still narrow it to certain trials.
  • Using Google Sheets? In your sheet choose File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx), drop that file here, then open the result in Google Sheets with File → Import.
  • Balloted trials: drop your trial spreadsheet (after Trial Lists) and choose A balloted trial. Pick the accepted entrants or the waitlist for each trial that was balloted. The waitlist tab lists every dog with its waitlist number, in order. For other lists from a trial spreadsheet, pick the Master List tab: dogs on a waitlist are never counted as entered in that trial.
  • Duplicates are matched ignoring capitals and stray spaces, so Jo@Mail.com and jo@mail.com count as one person. A couple sharing an address get one row with both names, like “Sam Lee / Alex Lee”.
  • The file never leaves your computer. All the work happens in this page.
